# Ticket 830673 / visit 6756 — a manifest link that contradicts itself

**Status: OPEN. Needs a human decision. Nothing in the database has been changed.**
Found 2026-08-24 while cross-validating `rpa-derm-monthly` against the database.

**Who can close this:** Fred, with Diego and Mark Noltion. It is a Miami-Dade compliance
record, not a data-cleanup task, and the deciding facts are not in the database.

---

## The contradiction in one line

Visit **6756** (175-PV Pura Vida Brickell 701) is dated **2026-07-29** and is linked to white
manifest **830673**, which was dumped **2026-07-22**. The pickup is dated **seven days after its
own offload**. Grease is pumped before it is dumped, so one of the two records is wrong.

It reaches Jonathan's Miami-Dade **Liquid Waste Transporter** monthly filing through
`derm.v_lwt_monthly_rows` → `rpa-derm-monthly`, where it reads as waste offloaded before it was
collected.

## The evidence points both ways. That is why this is open.

### Saying the grease was NOT collected on that load

1. **The Jobber note.** *"Couldn't compete the job because the PTO had broken."* The power take-off
   drives the vacuum pump. If it failed, nothing could be pumped.
2. **The business itself reversed the completion.** `jobber-daily-completion-reconcile` set
   `completed_at` back to NULL on 07-29 because Jobber showed the job had not been done. Only 4 such
   reversals exist in the entire audit history.
3. **The job was re-run on 07-29** and completed 07-30 06:02, with a second independent completion tap.
4. **The other four visits on the load are consistent and 6756 is not:**
   7089 065-TCE 07-13 · 6145 068-TCE 07-20 · 6257 092-TCE 07-20 · 6018 036-LG 07-20 — all before the
   07-22 dump.
5. **Precedent.** Visit **7090** (112-YA) was un-completed by the same cron on 2026-07-30, is now
   `skipped`, and had **already been unlinked by hand**. When a linked visit turns out not to have
   happened, the link is what gets removed.

### Saying it WAS collected

1. **The paper white manifest 830673 lists it.** Generator #2: `GDO-02560 · 175-PV Pura Vida
   Brickell 701 · 701 Brickell Avenue, Miami, Florida, 33131`. This is the document that went to the
   county. Scan: `manifests/derm/1625/address_1.JPG`.
2. **The dump ticket is signed and specific.** No. 830673, South District WWTP, 7/22/2026, 2000
   gallons, $188, delivered by **Mark Noltion**, received by Devereaux Booth at **00:52**. Scan:
   `manifests/derm/1625/manifest_1.JPG`.
3. **The load was dumped at 00:52 on 07-22**, so the collecting run was the night of 07-21/22 — two
   days *after* the PTO failure. The truck could have been repaired and the route re-run, with Pura
   Vida pumped then. In that reading the paper is right and it is the **visit record** that is
   incomplete: there was a service on 07-21/22 that was never recorded, and the 07-29 visit is a
   second, separate service.

### What the paper cannot settle

Every per-generator column on the FOG eManifest is **blank** — FOG control device type, capacity,
wet volume, all the measurements, and the per-generator gallons and date. Someone drew a large "FOG"
across section C instead of filling it. Only the facility total is written (2000 gal = the truck's
full stated capacity, from section A).

So the paper proves Pura Vida was **listed on the run**. It does not prove Pura Vida was **pumped**.
A generator list written at the start of a route and a record of what actually came off each trap
are not the same document, and here they are the same blank grid.

## The two outcomes, and what each costs

**If Pura Vida was NOT pumped onto that load** — the link is wrong. Remove it and keep both sides:

```sql
-- back this up to backups/ first, and read the consequences below
DELETE FROM public.manifest_visits WHERE manifest_id = 1628 AND visit_id = 6756;
```

Manifest row 1628 stays (175-PV is printed on the physical sheet; deleting our copy would erase the
record of what was filed) and visit 6756 stays with its date untouched. Follows the precedent of
`2026-07-15_unlink_009cn_impossible_manifest_visit.sql`. **Consequences:** the 2026-07 LWT report
drops from 90 to 89 in-scope rows, and it then exposes that 175-PV's real 07-29 pickup has **no
filed manifest at all**. Note `trg_ac_link_visit_not_after_dump` will refuse to let this link be
re-inserted afterwards (verified 2026-08-24), so the rollback needs the trigger disabled.

**If Pura Vida WAS pumped on 07-21/22** — the paper is right and the visit record is incomplete.
Then the fix is on the visits side: a service on 07-21/22 was never recorded, and someone needs to
decide whether to create it. **Do not** simply move visit 6756's date back to 07-19: the 07-29
service demonstrably happened, and overwriting it would erase a real second service and silently
re-bless a completion tap the business itself reversed.

**Either way, do not clamp the dates in `derm.v_lwt_monthly_rows` or `rpa-derm-monthly`.** That was
considered and rejected on 2026-08-24: hiding an anomaly on a regulator-facing report is worse than
printing one.

## What WAS shipped on 2026-08-24, and what it does and does not cover

Migration `2026-08-24_1510_manifest_link_completed_visit_guard.sql`:

- **`trg_ad_link_visit_completed`** on `public.manifest_visits` — a link may not be made against a
  visit that has not happened. ⚠ **This would NOT have stopped this row**: visit 6756 *was* marked
  completed on 07-22. It is defence in depth for the adjacent hole, and it costs nothing (0 of 690
  current links violate it).
- **`trg_ae_dump_date_keeps_links_valid`** on `public.derm_manifests` — the manifest side of the same
  invariant, and it also closes the NULL → value hole that `trg_ac` deliberately allows.
- **`derm.v_manifest_link_date_conflicts`** — the detector, and the **only** thing that covers this
  case. Its `visit_not_completed` conflict kind fires the moment a reconciler reverses a completion
  on a linked visit, which here was 13:46 on 07-29.
- **`rpa-derm-monthly` now returns `data_quality.conflicts`** and an inline `anomaly` on the affected
  row, so this cannot be filed unknowingly. The row is still returned, never hidden.

**The visits side is deliberately not blocked.** The writer that broke this link was an automated
reconciler recording the truth that the job had not happened. Blocking it would force the database
to keep asserting a service that never occurred. See the migration header for the full reasoning and
the measurements behind it.
